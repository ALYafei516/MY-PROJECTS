The Knowledge Sharing System (FK-EduSearch) will have several significant impacts:

a) Improved Learning Outcomes: Expert supervision and support will help students, 
resulting in increased research abilities, a deeper comprehension of course-related issues, 
and improved academic achievement. Access to a specialised information-sharing 
platform will assist successful learning and knowledge acquisition.

b) Collaborative Environment: FK-EduSearch will encourage student and expert 
cooperation inside the department or institution. Students may communicate with 
classmates, exchange views, and receive feedback from professionals via the discussion 
board and interactions. This partnership will foster critical thinking, creativity, and 
innovation.

c) Enhanced Expertise and Professional Development: The website allows lecturers and 
employees to exhibit their knowledge, extend their professional networks, and remain up 
to speed on the most recent research and trends. Experts can improve their teaching 
abilities and contribute to their own professional growth by actively engaging with pupils.

d) Efficient Knowledge Sharing: FK-EduSearch simplifies the process of asking questions, 
obtaining answers, and offering comments. This guarantees that students receive timely 
and correct advice from professionals, removing the need for students to seek through 
different resources and saving time in obtaining important information.

e) Transparent Evaluation and Feedback: Students can submit ratings and feedback for 
designated experts using FK-EduSearch's rating and feedback system. This encourages 
openness and accountability, allowing students to make informed decisions and 
professionals to improve their teaching approaches and adapt to the requirements of 
students.

f) Data-Driven Decision Making: The reporting and analytics section provides useful 
insights into user activity, engagement rates, and user satisfaction. This information may 
be used to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify areas for development, and 
make educated decisions to improve the overall user experience.

g) Continuous Improvement: The complaint module allows users to submit any difficulties 
or concerns they have, ensuring that the admin hears and addresses their criticism. This 
fosters a culture of continual improvement, allowing the platform to grow in response to 
user demands and preferences.
